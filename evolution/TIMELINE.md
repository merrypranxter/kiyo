# Evolution Timeline

## 2016: Genesis

- **First sketch:** December 16, 2016
- **Style:** Simple geometric primitives, basic motion
- **Key techniques:** Simple particle systems, basic drawing
- **Principles from this era:**
  - [[DETERMINISTIC_FRAME_RANDOM]] - early use of seeded random
  - [[FFMPEG_CAPTURE_PIPELINE]] - established recording workflow

## 2017-2018: Exploration

- **Output:** ~730 sketches
- **Style:** Experimentation with forms, introducing noise and physics
- **New patterns:** Grid-based compositions, color exploration
- **Principles from this era:**
  - [[FRAME_SYNCED_SPIRAL_SPAWN]] - uzumaki and spiral forms
  - [[CIRCLE_PACKING_GROWTH]] - organic packing behaviors

## 2019-2020: Refinement

- **Output:** ~731 sketches (2020 was a leap year)
- **Style:** More sophisticated compositions, blend mode mastery
- **New patterns:** Temporal techniques, phased animations
- **Principles from this era:**
  - [[MODULO_SPAWN_PHASE_SHIFT]] - rhythmic spawn patterns
  - [[STEPPED_LIFETIME_ALPHA]] - deliberate fade curves
  - [[BLEND_MODE_MOOD_MAPPING]] - emotional rendering choices

## 2021-2022: Complexity

- **Output:** ~730 sketches
- **Style:** 3D forms, topology experiments, richer visual language
- **New patterns:** Torus knots, hexagonal structures, text manipulation
- **Principles from this era:**
  - [[RAMPAGING_TORUS_KNOT]] - chaotic topology
  - [[ALTERNATE_TORUS_FLIP]] - inside-out surfaces
  - [[HEXAGONAL_TILE_MORPH]] - tessellation deformation
  - [[MOSAIC_RECONSTRUCTION]] - tile assembly

## 2023-2024: Mastery

- **Output:** ~731 sketches (2024 was a leap year)
- **Style:** Confident combinations, physics integration, emotional depth
- **New patterns:** Gravity simulations, perspective illusions, generative typography
- **Principles from this era:**
  - [[DROP_RING_GRAVITY]] - physics-based playfulness
  - [[TUNNEL_PERSPECTIVE]] - spatial depth illusions
  - [[PRINT_TEXT_GENERATIVE]] - typography as geometry
  - [[ARROW_FIELD_FLOW]] - directional flow fields

## 2025-2026: Current

- **Output:** ~470+ sketches and counting
- **Style:** Refined combinations, daily discipline, open sharing
- **New patterns:** Deformation, ribbon dynamics, heart forms, strobe animation
- **Principles from this era:**
  - [[SWELL_DEFORMATION]] - organic breathing motion
  - [[RIBBON_TWIST_DEFORM]] - graceful path following
  - [[HEART_GENERATIVE]] - parametric sentiment
  - [[TWO_FRAME_ANIMATION]] - retro digital aesthetics
  - [[PROXIMITY_THRESHOLD_CONNECTION]] - ephemeral networks
  - [[NOISE_MASKED_GEOMETRY]] - selective visibility
  - [[BIDIRECTIONAL_ORBITAL_TRAILS]] - counter-motion poetry
  - [[VOXEL_GRID_THRESHOLD]] - emergent architecture

  - [[WIREFRAME_OVERLAY_BLEND]] - blueprint aesthetic

## Patterns of Evolution

1. **From 2D to 3D** - Early work is flat, recent work explores depth
2. **From random to deterministic** - Increasing use of controlled randomness
3. **From simple to combined** - Early principles in isolation, now layered
4. **From visual to emotional** - Recent work prioritizes mood over novelty
