## Unique to Kiyoshi

The following principles appear to be distinctively Kiyoshi's approach:

1. **[[BLEND_MODE_MOOD_MAPPING]]** - Using blend mode as primary emotional signal
2. **[[TWO_FRAME_ANIMATION]]** - Deliberately harsh 2-frame aesthetic
3. **[[FFMPEG_CAPTURE_PIPELINE]]** - Frame-perfect recording as creative workflow
4. **[[DETERMINISTIC_FRAME_RANDOM]]** - Per-frame seeded chaos (unusual to reseed every frame)
5. **[[BIDIRECTIONAL_ORBITAL_TRAILS]]** - Fat circle trails (overlapping circles, not lines)
